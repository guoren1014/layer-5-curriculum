Section 05

Things to Consider

These are implementation decisions for team review, not unresolved curriculum architecture.

Curated pilot dataset

Keep the curriculum dataset-agnostic until a shared dataset is selected and cleared.

Research brief format

Use a common 1–2 page brief or equivalent digital format for the synchronous pilot.

Simulation grouping

Use groups of roughly 3–5 and combine reviewer lenses when needed.

Guest speaker

Select collaboratively with the co-design team; treat the guest as enrichment, not a dependency.

Release Gate use

Use as a quality decision and revision guide rather than a high-stakes numeric score.

AI participation

AI remains optional and privacy-safe; instructor-provided examples support restricted environments.

Public portfolio expectation

Portfolio-ready does not automatically mean public-ready. Release depends on governance and data constraints.

Competency reporting

Use developmental evidence language: Apply-level evidence and emerging Design-level evidence, not certification.

After approval: Curriculum → learner-facing syllabus → facilitator guide → learner templates → slides → worked-case assets → asynchronous conversion.
